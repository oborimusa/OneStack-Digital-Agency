Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our process and pricing.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. We require a 50% deposit to start. The remaining 50% is due on project completion before we hand over the final files.

What about hosting and domain?

We recommend hosting providers and can help you set up. Hosting costs are separate and range from ₦50,000–₦200,000/year depending on your needs.

How long does a project take?

Starter and Business packages: 2 weeks. Premium: 3 weeks. This depends on content delivery from your side.

Do you redesign existing websites?

Yes. We can redesign your current website. Same packages apply – we'll analyze your existing site and give you a custom quote.

What about ongoing maintenance?

After your free support period, we offer monthly maintenance plans starting at ₦50,000/month. This includes updates, backups, and security monitoring.

Can I upgrade later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade from Starter to Business or Premium at any time. We'll credit your previous investment toward the upgrade.

Common Questions

What real estate clients ask before we start.

Can I add properties myself?

Yes — with the Business package you get an admin dashboard. You log in, upload photos, add prices, and the property goes live instantly. No developer needed.

How long does it take?

Starter: 2 weeks. Business: 3 weeks. The biggest delay is usually waiting for you to send property photos and descriptions — if you have those ready, we can move fast.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain, the code, the hosting, the analytics — all in your name from day one. If you ever want to move to a different developer, you can. No lock-in.

What if I don't have great photos yet?

We can launch with placeholder images and you replace them as you get professional shoots. Or we can recommend a property photographer in your area.

Do you also run Google Ads?

Yes — we can set up and manage Google Ads for your property listings. That's usually a separate monthly retainer, starting from ₦80,000/month plus ad spend.

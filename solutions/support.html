The Problem

What happens when a Nigerian business ignores their website for months.

01

It slows down — and you don't notice

Google and your visitors both notice. Slower sites rank lower, and every extra second costs you conversions. But you won't know until you check.

02

Security holes open up

Every day your site runs on outdated software is another day attackers look for a way in. Most Nigerian SME sites are hacked through known, fixable vulnerabilities.

03

Content gets stale

Prices change, staff change, services change — but your website still says 2023. Customers lose trust when what they see online doesn't match reality.

04

Nobody's watching

If your site goes down at 2 AM on a Saturday, you find out when a customer tells you on Monday. By then, you've lost bookings, enquiries, and revenue.

Common Questions

What Nigerian business owners ask before signing up.

What if I don't have a OneStackDA website?

We support sites built by us, but we also support sites built by other developers — WordPress, custom code, whatever. As long as we can access the code and hosting, we can support it.

What counts as "content changes"?

Text updates, image swaps, price changes, adding new pages (up to a point), fixing small layout issues, updating forms, and anything else short of a full redesign. If it takes under 30 minutes, it's included.

Do you have a long-term contract?

No. Month-to-month. Cancel any time with 30 days' notice. We keep clients because we're useful — not because we locked them in.

What happens if my site is hacked?

We clean it immediately, patch the vulnerability, and restore from the most recent clean backup. Malware cleanup is included in all plans — no extra charge.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Yes, any time. Just message us. Changes take effect the next billing cycle.

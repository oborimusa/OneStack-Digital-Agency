Common Questions

What Nigerian restaurant owners ask before we start.

Can I update my menu myself?

Yes. The Business package includes an admin dashboard where you add, edit, or remove menu items, change prices, and mark items as unavailable — all without calling a developer.

How do customers pay for online orders?

We integrate Paystack or Flutterwave — the two most trusted payment gateways in Nigeria. Customers pay with card, bank transfer, or USSD. The money goes directly to your account.

Do you handle delivery?

We don't deliver food, but we can integrate with third-party delivery services, or set up a "pickup only" option if you prefer to handle logistics yourself.

Can customers still order on Glovo/Chowdeck?

Yes — nothing stops you from running both. Many restaurants use apps for discovery and their own site for repeat customers and larger orders, where commission hurts most.

How long does it take?

Starter: 2 weeks. Business: 3 weeks. The biggest delay is usually waiting for your menu items, prices, and food photography. If those are ready, we move fast.

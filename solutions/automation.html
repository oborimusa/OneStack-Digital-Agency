Common Questions

What Nigerian business owners ask before automating.

Will WhatsApp ban my number for using a bot?

No, as long as we use the official WhatsApp Business API. If you need a bot on your personal WhatsApp, we can use WhatsApp Business (not the personal app) which allows automation without violating their terms.

Do I need technical skills to use this?

No. Everything we build has a simple interface — either a WhatsApp conversation, a web dashboard, or a mobile-friendly form. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use our automations.

How long does it take to build?

Simple bots: 2 weeks. Business workflows: 3 weeks. Custom systems: 4–8 weeks, depending on complexity. We give you a firm timeline before starting.

What if I need to change something later?

Small changes are included in the first 30 days. After that, we offer monthly support plans (₦50K–₦200K) that cover ongoing changes, fixes, and improvements.

Will this replace my staff?

No — it replaces the repetitive tasks your staff shouldn't be doing. They keep doing what humans do best: building relationships, solving problems, growing the business.

The Problem

Nigerian hotels are losing money to platforms they don't own.

01

Commissions eating your margin

Booking platforms take 15–20% of every reservation. On a ₦50,000 room, that's ₦10,000 gone. Every single booking. That's money your hotel needs.

02

Guests can't find you directly

If someone hears about your hotel and searches for it on Google, they land on a third-party listing — not your site. You lose control of the guest relationship from day one.

03

No photos, no story, no trust

Guests decide where to stay based on what they see online. If your only online presence is a basic listing, you look like every other option.

04

Every enquiry is manual

Guests call or WhatsApp. You tell them availability. They ask questions. Both of you waste time. Bookings that could have happened instantly get lost.

Common Questions

What Nigerian hotel owners ask before we start.

Can I manage bookings myself?

Yes. The Business package includes an admin dashboard where you see all bookings, confirm or cancel them, and reply to guest enquiries — all from one screen.

Does this replace Booking.com?

Not necessarily — but it reduces your dependence on them. Many hotels run both: listings on Booking.com for discovery, and their own site for repeat guests and direct bookings (where you keep 100% of the money).

How do guests pay?

We integrate Paystack, Flutterwave, or direct bank transfer. Guests pay a deposit or full amount at booking. The money goes directly to your account — we never touch it.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. If you can use WhatsApp and email, you can use our admin dashboard. And we provide 30 days of free support after launch — call or WhatsApp us any time.

Can you also run Google Ads for my hotel?

Yes. We can set up and manage Google Ads targeting travellers searching for hotels in your area. Typically a monthly retainer from ₦80,000/month plus ad spend.
